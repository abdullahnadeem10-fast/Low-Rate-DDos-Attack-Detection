The original model/evaluation freeze remains unchanged. The matrix is two slow mechanisms × one/three sources × two connection/timing profiles × five repetitions; benign ordinary/bursts/flash/long-lived-congested each have five repetitions. This adds an application path compared with development, so transfer/application-path limitations remain; it does not repair sparse V or prove generalization.

The first partial second and observation tails stay unknown. No corroborated initial payload means unknown attack measurement plus a retained unscorable planned episode, never a fabricated hit/miss or benign label. Record all failed/ineffective units; do not recollect or tune intensity to improve results.
