# Phase 3 — Progress and handoff

## 2026-11-04: Started the offline pilot

- Created `phase 3`, this `plan` folder, a live checklist, source scripts and an evidence directory. The local `.venv` runs on Windows; no system software was installed. WSL is not installed and the live lab has not been set up.
- Used the [UNB publisher documentation](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html) for the schedule, endpoint inventory and dataset citation. The official download page returned a form/server error; the pilot used a public mirror pinned to revision `811c007f08d693a8a8b4226c197c138391806ce6`. The Monday file was requested with HTTP Range `0-33554431`; the server returned `206` and the matching Content-Range. Only that benign prefix was parsed. The partial trailing 100 bytes were discarded so the packet reader received complete blocks.
- The synthetic fixture passed checks for packet direction, captured byte counts, idle seconds, history reset and lack of future influence. The real input covers 635.357 seconds and contains 54,632 readable frames. The chosen active benign LDAP endpoint contributed 2,516 TCP frames, producing 630 one-second feature windows. The planned HTTP/HTTPS endpoints did not appear in the prefix. The processing script reported 3.941 seconds including the smoke model, excluding process startup and download; not an extrapolation to a full capture.
- A benign-only Isolation Forest fit, calibration and scoring pass completed on chronological blocks of the same Monday prefix, separated by five-second gaps. It emitted 6 alerts in 153 later windows. This is a pipeline diagnostic with one service and one capture; it is not a final FPR, not the Phase 2 hybrid or full calibration protocol, and says nothing about attack recall or real-time delay. The 22 out-of-order timestamps can be aggregated offline by event time; live handling of late packets remains to be designed and tested.
- The full flow-label ZIP matched the mirror's SHA-256 and is stored under `data/quarantine/`. The audit opened **only** the allowlisted Friday DDoS CSV member. It counted 97,718 benign and 128,027 DDoS rows. All 225,745 timestamps are minute-only, 50,023 directed five-tuple/time keys repeat, and no exact key had conflicting labels in this member. The attack timestamps as written span `03:56` to `04:16`, while UNB's schedule lists the attack in the afternoon. Treat the apparent 12-hour difference as a hypothesis, not a validated clock conversion. Duplicate keys are not by themselves proof of duplicate flows. No packet labels have been assigned from these values.
- Wednesday raw captures and the external dataset were not fetched. Other members of the flow-label archive, including Wednesday, were not opened for development. No live attack traffic was generated and no final model was trained.

## 2026-11-09: Continued development and label verification

- Implemented a bounded-lateness packet replay with 30 causal predictors, 5/10/20-second history and a 30-second connection inactivity timeout. It tracks observed TCP handshakes, connection age/missing age, expiry, direction and timing. Metadata identifiers and label/CSV totals are excluded from its predictors. Its causal feature core, state resets and late-packet handling are checked by protocol fixtures. The Monday prefix replay produced 595 service windows, 576 with complete 20-second history, and dropped no selected late packets. These counts differ from the first pilot because the new replay starts when the service is first observed and requires a longer warm-up.
- Implemented Friday-only, resumable acquisition plus full-file SHA-256 verification. Kept the slow-family and external raw captures untouched. Saved partial ranges can be reused; never accept an assembled capture without the final expected hash.
- A bounded tail replay retained original PCAPNG interface metadata and validated complete packet blocks. It contains 425,888 frames. After canonical bidirectional grouping, 8,656 unambiguous benign keys corroborated a **+15-hour combined offset** between the printed CSV clock and packet UTC timestamps, with no competing offset in that sample. This supports missing PM notation plus a three-hour conversion to UTC; do not reuse the correction for other datasets without auditing them. The full-capture audit is the confirmation step, not yet implied by the bounded result.
- Found two negative-duration benign rows. The packet join quarantines the complete two affected connection keys, including two other valid rows sharing those keys, and records the exclusions. Raw class counts are preserved. Flow orientation is canonicalized so reverse-oriented rows do not get treated as different connection identities.
- Ran an explicitly separate **completed-flow development** comparison on the permitted Friday CSV. Fitting used 27,691 rows; score reference C1 used 1,712 benign rows; C2 used 1,023 benign rows; development V used 44,741 rows. Canonical connection keys are disjoint across these partitions, minute-start uncertainty is considered at boundaries, and later records are not scored. The script removed 12,515 candidate rows with cross-partition keys and two impossible durations.
- The auxiliary comparison trained Logistic Regression, Random Forest, HGB, a benign-only dense AE and HGB/AE score fusion. The AE used its full 100-epoch cap. At C2-calibrated thresholds, HGB and fusion both detected 34,685 of 34,700 known DDoS V flows; fusion had 11 V false positives against HGB's 10. There is **no demonstrated hybrid advantage** here. AE recall was approximately 62.8% at its calibrated operating point despite a high ranking score. These are one-seed, one-episode **development flow** outcomes, not final packet-window research results or evidence about slow/unseen attacks.
- The full Friday capture finished acquisition and matched SHA-256 `beff0dcce1eebc9b2454582f4dc8ed0ba0112b2c619a710bf03af93147254cd0` at 8,839,309,056 bytes. Acquisition took 5,414.94 seconds on this connection, including retries/assembly; that is not feature-processing time. The full clock pass read 9,997,874 frames, observed 7,094 adjacent timestamp regressions and confirmed the +15-hour correction with 22,768 benign keys (largest competing offset: 40 keys). Do not transfer this correction to another capture without auditing it.
- The full join retained 86,419 canonical groups after quarantine: 33,486 benign and 25,278 DDoS groups had exact directional-count agreement. There were 7,369 benign and 59 DDoS mismatches, 20,055 conflicting BENIGN/DDoS groups, and 172 unseen benign groups. These are aggregate canonical-key comparisons over possible minute-resolution intervals, not exact flow reconstruction. The target's 1,280,602 packets inside the label envelope included 488,376 corroborated attack, 1,160 corroborated benign and 791,066 uncorroborated packets.
- Full Friday feature replay produced 18,894 one-second `.50:80` windows, including 18,875 with complete 20-second history, from 1,282,861 selected TCP packets. There were zero selected late-packet drops and zero decode errors. Replay took 261.434 seconds on this PC, excluding startup/download and without live latency measurement. All 1,096 overlapping label seconds agreed exactly with replay packet counts; all predictors were finite and output seconds contiguous.
- **Readiness finding:** 1,076 windows contain corroborated attack packets, 20 have uncertain labels, and 17,798 are unlabelled; **zero are fully corroborated benign-only**. Of all replay windows, 17,470 are empty. Silence is not automatically a benign label. The auxiliary CSV negatives span other services and do not solve matched HTTP benign calibration; service/traffic confounding remains possible. Do not train a headline packet detector on this alone.
- Saved `development_manifest.json`, `friday_packet_readiness.json`, replay/audit evidence and the 18-test passing validation summary/log under `results/development/`. The continuous replay has not been allocated into split-safe training partitions. `training_ready` is deliberately false. `NEXT_STEPS.md` records the ordered development gate. No slow-family or external raw source was fetched/opened, no live attack traffic was generated, and no final model was frozen.

The full Friday audit and replay are complete engineering milestones. Compatible benign packet coverage, packet-window group integration, benign-stress lab calibration, final freeze, withheld slow-family tests and external transfer remain unchecked until their own evidence exists. The fixture checks and auxiliary flow experiment do not replace them.
